# Market Positioning: AI-native AUTOSAR security integration
*Skill: sk-positioning | Generated: 2026-10-25 | Mode: Deep | Status: complete (verification below)*

## Positioning (3-Sentence Summary)
[Company] integrates an ECU's AUTOSAR security software (crypto stack, SecOC/KeyM, HSM, secure boot, secure flashing and diagnostics) on the customer's own Vector or ETAS stack, verifies it on the target, and delivers the ISO 21434 / ASPICE evidence with it, signed by named senior engineers. It is for BSW and cybersecurity managers at mid-size Tier 1s and OEM ECU teams facing an R155 or GB 44495 evidence deadline without a spare HSM expert. It wins because nobody else is accountable for the seam between silicon, stack and evidence: vendors secure their own products, freelancers sell hours, tools write documents.

**Strength: Moderate.** The rung is open and the founders have shipped this exact work, but the track record belongs to two individuals, and two proof points (controlled-AI evidence, published accuracy) still have to be built.

## Positioning Statement
**Moore:** For BSW and cybersecurity managers at mid-size Tier 1 suppliers and OEM ECU teams who must deliver an ECU's security stack and its R155 / ISO 21434 evidence by a fixed deadline without a spare HSM expert, [Company] is an AUTOSAR security integration provider that delivers the crypto stack, SecOC, HSM and secure boot working on your target, on your existing Vector or ETAS stack, with the ISO 21434 and ASPICE evidence included and signed by named senior engineers. Unlike renting a security freelancer or queuing for the stack vendor's consultants, we sell a fixed-scope, fixed-price package with acceptance on target, and we own the seam between silicon, stack and evidence.

**Onliness (Neumeier):** [Company] is the only AUTOSAR security integration provider we know of that delivers your ECU's security stack verified on target and its ISO 21434 / ASPICE evidence, as one fixed-price package signed by named engineers. *(Pass with conditions: keep "we know of"; scope to Vector and ETAS on Infineon until more stacks are delivered.)*

**Tagline (recommended):** "Your ECU's security stack, working on target. Evidence included."

## Competitive Alternatives
- **Most often met:** a senior security freelancer (EUR 85-120/h), the overloaded internal expert / slipping schedule, and deferring security scope or accepting the audit finding.
- **Credibility comparisons:** stack vendor services (Vector, ETAS/ESCRYPT, EB), large ESPs (KPIT, Tata Elxsi, LTTS, Capgemini, DXC/Luxoft), security product vendors with services (FESCARO, Autocrypt), Cairo ESPs (Avelabs).
- **Adjacent:** captive centres, DIY with coding agents + Vector MCP, MCU vendor reference software, AI-TARA tools, body leasing.
- **The gap:** no alternative is accountable for making security work across silicon, stack and evidence at once.

## Market Category
**AUTOSAR security integration**: subcategory (big fish, small pond) where automotive cybersecurity meets BSW integration. Regulation-forced (R155/R156, GB 44495). "Secure ECU package" is the candidate offer name for Phase 5. Expansion frame (year 2+): evidence-included AUTOSAR BSW integration packages. Investor frame: Services-as-Software for safety- and security-critical automotive software.

## Value Themes
1. **The security stack works on your ECU, whichever vendors are in it:** on-target integration across the silicon vendor's MCAL and the BSW vendor's stack (proven: Vector MICROSAR on AURIX TC3xx / TRAVEO; ETAS RTA with ETAS HSM on AURIX, confirmed by Hesham 2026-10-25).
2. **Evidence your OEM and assessor accept, signed by people who can defend it:** ISO 21434 and ASPICE SWE / SEC evidence included; named engineers ready for the assessor's comprehension questions.
3. **A fixed scope and price you can plan before the deadline:** one bounded package per ECU with an acceptance test, fit for headcount freezes and "selective adoption".

## Best-Fit Customer
A mid-size Tier 1 (about 1-10 secure ECU programmes a year) or OEM ECU team that: has an R155 flow-down or GB 44495 carry-over evidence deadline within 6-12 months; runs Vector MICROSAR or ETAS RTA on Infineon; has no spare in-house HSM / security-BSW expert and today rents a freelancer; is under ASPICE L2/L3 pressure; already buys from best-cost locations. First revenue path: approved ESPs or staffing primes that subcontract the package. Poor fit: large Tier 1s with captives and master ESP contracts.

## Trend Overlay
Primary: **assessor-controlled AI** (assessors now ask "is that use controlled?"; only 5% of automotive organisations have mature AI governance). Use only after the controlled-AI harness exists. Secondary, offer-level: **GB 44495 carry-over deadline (1 Jan 2028)**.

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"You know the ECU where SecOC, the HSM and secure boot all have to work together on target, and the OEM wants the 21434 evidence in its format by a date that won't move? That's what we do, and only that. We integrate the security stack on your Vector or ETAS setup, verify it on your hardware, and hand over the ASPICE and 21434 work products with it, signed by the two of us. I've done this on ADAS, inverter and lidar ECUs; my brother owns the SWE.2 architecture side. It's a fixed price per ECU, not a freelancer by the hour."

## Decisions and Changes vs Earlier Phases
- **Lead with the security wedge (Silver H) as the company's positioning**, not only as the first offer. The Gold-niche promise "ASPICE-ready AUTOSAR BSW in half the time and cost" is held back: no baseline, and the broad BSW frame is weak during the 2026 ER&D trough.
- **AI is the method, not the headline.** "AI-native" is the most crowded rung (5 of 9 competitor homepages).
- **Claims scoped to proven ground** (Vector + ETAS on Infineon); new stacks/MCUs enter after delivery (founders plan hires).
- **ISO 21434 evidence stays in the promise, conditional on a named 21434/TARA hire or partner** before it appears in buyer-facing copy (founder decision 2026-10-25; the founders' proven evidence work is ASPICE SWE.2/SWE.3).
- **Proof points to build first:** controlled-AI evidence harness mapped to the four intacs axes; a published accuracy/rework benchmark on one reference package, pre-assessed by an independent intacs assessor.

## Key Flags
- **Red:** no company track record; NDA / non-solicit exposure (do not name former employers' customers or programmes before legal review); controlled-AI and metrics not built; the "only" rests on packaging; end-of-line key provisioning not in the founders' record; ISO 21434 cybersecurity-case / TARA and SEC.1-4 authorship not in the founders' record, although "21434 evidence included" is in every statement (see verification report).
- **Yellow:** small, unsized pond; HITL language becoming table stakes by 2027; no formal intacs/VDA position on AI evidence; GB 44495 extension risk; Cairo talent competition.
