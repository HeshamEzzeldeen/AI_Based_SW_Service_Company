### Overused or hollow words (claimed without proof) [Opinion, M]
- **"AI-native" / "agentic"**: Tata Elxsi, LTTS, KPIT, Capgemini and DXC all use it. None publishes accuracy, defect rates or a measurement method. Buyers already discount it: only 40% of automotive executives trust agentic AI for development (Phase 3).
- **"Up to X% faster / cheaper"**: Luxoft AMBER 50%/30%, EB 50%/30%, VxLabs 85%, Ketryx 90%. Always phrased "up to", with no stated baseline.
- **"Partner"** (strategic, preferred, key): describes a relationship without naming a deliverable.
- **"Human-in-the-loop / experts stay in control / governed"**: emerging fast (VxLabs, LTTS "governed agentic", Capgemini "humans sign it", Tata Elxsi). It will be table stakes by 2027.

## 4. Key insights for positioning

1. **"AI-native" no longer differentiates; it is the 2026 default** [Data, H]. Five of eleven competitors put AI in their headline or title (Tata Elxsi "AI-First", LTTS "AI-led", DXC "AI-powered SI", KPIT "AI-Defined Vehicles", EB "AI-centric"). Leading with "AI-native" puts the founders in the most crowded rung, against firms with 10,000+ staff.
2. **Vector's restraint is a signal** [Opinion, M]. The incumbent with the most real agentic capability avoids the word "AI" on its homepage and says "intelligent foundation". In safety-critical buying, understated, proof-led language reads as more credible than "AI-first". Lead with outcomes and evidence; let AI explain *how* the price is possible.
3. **The intent-perception gap is widest at Tata Elxsi and Luxoft** [Opinion, M]. Tata Elxsi claims AI-native but reads as a design and media generalist (ISG Leader in only 2 quadrants). The Luxoft specialist brand is being absorbed into DXC (redirect observed). Both openings concern *specialist credibility*, which a narrow positioning can take.
4. **KPIT's perception is "SDV integration specialist + margin story"** [Data, H]. Its fixed price is real, but its AI message goes to investors. "Your AI savings, on the invoice" is a contrast no one else can easily copy without hurting their own margin narrative.
5. **"Agents build it, humans sign it" is already industry vocabulary (Capgemini), but nobody sells it** [Data, H / Opinion]. The founders can borrow its legitimacy and productise it: named reviewer, defined checkpoints, findings fixed at their own cost.
6. **Cybersecurity is a headline topic, but nobody sells implementation** [Data, H]. EB's hero is "Cybersecurity is no longer optional", VxLabs sells AI TARA, ESCRYPT sells consulting. The rung "we implement and verify the security BSW on your ECU across your vendors" is open, and the market already cares.
7. **Every "% faster" claim in the market is "up to" with no baseline** [Data, H]. Publishing a method and a real benchmark, even a modest one (the credible range is 10-19%, Phase 3), would be unique. This supports rewording the unproven "half the time and cost" promise.
8. **The analyst lens rewards breadth and scale** (ISG 2026: 48 providers; Leaders are all large SIs) [Data, H]. Category-level "SDV services" positioning would place the founders at the bottom of a matrix built for others. A narrow, new sub-category avoids that comparison.
