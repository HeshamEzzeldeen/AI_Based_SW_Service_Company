## Messaging Hierarchy
1. **Outcome:** the ECU's security stack works on target, and the evidence is accepted. ("Your ECU's security stack, working on target. Evidence included.")
2. **Time / deadline:** before your OEM security review, SOP or the GB 44495 date (1 Jan 2028), without pulling your senior integrators.
3. **Key differentiator:** on your existing stack (Vector MICROSAR or ETAS RTA, on Infineon AURIX/TRAVEO), signed by named senior engineers who can sit the assessor interview.
4. **Cost / access:** fixed scope, fixed price per ECU package, with an acceptance test on target. Can be contracted directly or through your approved ESP.
5. **Deliverables:** Csm/CryIf + crypto driver; SecOC, FVM, KeyM; HSM integration and HSM updater; secure boot (incl. OTA), secure flashing, secure diagnostics; ISO 21434 work products and ASPICE SWE / SEC.1-4 evidence traced to the implementation.
6. **Value anchor:** compared with a senior security freelancer for 6-12 months, or the stack vendor's consultants, with no evidence trail at the end.

## Category Label
**Always use:** "AUTOSAR security integration".
Use it on the website, LinkedIn company tagline, partner directories (Infineon, ESP partner pages), proposals and talk bios. Use "Secure ECU package" only as the offer name once Phase 5 confirms it.

## Value Anchor
**"What one senior security freelancer costs for six months, with the evidence and the accountability included."**
- Freelancer reference: DACH IT median EUR 95/h (range 80-120); embedded and AUTOSAR listings EUR 85/h [Data, M]. At EUR 85-120/h × 160 h that is about EUR 14-19k per month [Estimate, M]. Security specialists are estimated at EUR 120-180/h [Data, L, third-party estimate].
- Use in body copy, proposals and pricing pages. Do **not** use in headlines, and do not quote numbers publicly until Phase 7 sets the price.
- **Do not anchor on "half the cost"** (no baseline; Phase 3/7).
