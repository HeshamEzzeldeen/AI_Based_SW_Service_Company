# Luxoft (DXC Technology) Battle Card: AI-Native AUTOSAR BSW Delivery
*Skill: sk-competitors | Generated: 2026-10-24*

**Threat level: Medium** [Opinion]. Wide OEM reach and an existing "50% faster" claim, but cockpit-centric and under a pressured parent.

> **Legal check first.** 02-niches.md records that Waled may be bound by a Luxoft non-solicitation clause. Get legal review before approaching Luxoft customers or staff.

## At a Glance
- **What they do:** Global ESP inside DXC Technology (acquired 2019, >12 mo): digital cockpit, connected vehicle, AD; also configuration, integration and testing of safety-critical platform software (DXC claim). Claims it works with "80% of the world's automakers". [Data, company claims]
- **Scale:** DXC Engineering group 11,000+ engineers in 30 countries across industries; automotive-only DATA GAP. Revenue not broken out. [Data]
- **Price / model:** T&M / framework agreements; delivery from Eastern Europe (RO, PL, RS), India, LatAm (USD 35-95/h nearshore band). [Data + Estimate]
- **AI:** **AMBER** IVI software platform (CES 2026) with "embedded AI", claiming "up to 50% faster development" and "30% lower cost" (vendor claims); GenAI tool for AD scenario generation (Feb 2026). No BSW AI. [Data]
- **AUTOSAR:** only older job ads found; current BSW depth DATA GAP. [Data]
- **Best for:** OEMs wanting cockpit/IVI and AD engineering with Eastern European nearshore delivery. [Opinion]

## Their Strengths (be honest)
- **Very wide OEM reach** and long relationships (via DXC and CMORE acquisition for German OEM access, 2020, >12 mo). [Data]
- **Nearshore bench** in Eastern Europe, close to German buyers in time zone and culture. [Data]
- **Already uses your headline** ("up to 50% faster", "30% lower cost") with a branded platform. [Data]
- Employees cite "access to large-scale international projects ... automotive" and "solid engineering culture" (Glassdoor snippets). [Data, Tier 3]

## Their Weaknesses (your openings)
- **Not a BSW/security specialist**; focus is cockpit, IVI and AD. [Data + Opinion]
- **Inconsistent delivery across accounts:** "Each account has its own tools, processes, and expectations, which can lead to inconsistent engineering practices and onboarding complexity" (Glassdoor snippet, undated). [Data, Tier 3]
- **Account-driven layoffs and turnover:** "sudden layoffs whenever clients lost"; "no merit increase since 2022"; parent "very strict and outdated" (snippets). [Data, Tier 3]
- **Parent DXC under long-running financial pressure** may limit investment. [Assumption]

## How to Win Against Them
- **Lead with:** specialist, fixed-price security BSW package with consistent, tool-driven delivery (same agents, same checks, every project). Directly contrasts "inconsistent engineering practices". [Opinion]
- **Treat their "50% faster" as a vendor claim**, and show measured numbers from your own benchmark and pilot. [Opinion]
- **Talking point:** "Luxoft's 50% is for IVI on its own platform. Ours is measured on your Classic ECU security scope, and it's on the invoice." [Opinion]

## When They Win Over You
- Cockpit, IVI and AD programmes.
- German OEM accounts that want nearshore teams with physical proximity.
- Buyers with existing DXC/Luxoft frameworks who want one supplier.
- Any account covered by Waled's non-solicitation clause.

## Objections and Responses
- **"Luxoft also promises 50% faster."** Response: "For their IVI platform. Ask for the measurement method. Here is ours for security BSW." (Requires a benchmark.) [Opinion]
- **"Luxoft is nearshore; you're in Cairo."** Response: "Cairo is one hour from CET, with lower cost, and our price is fixed per ECU, so ramp-up risk is ours." [Data + Opinion]

## Their Customers' Top Complaint
Proxy (employee voice): "Project experience can vary significantly depending on the client ... inconsistent engineering practices and onboarding complexity" (Glassdoor snippet, undated). **This matters because:** it hints at ramp-up friction buyers feel; a tool-driven, repeatable delivery model addresses it. Unverified from the buyer side.
