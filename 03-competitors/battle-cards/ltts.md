# L&T Technology Services (LTTS) Battle Card: AI-Native AUTOSAR BSW Delivery
*Skill: sk-competitors | Generated: 2026-10-24*

**Threat level: Medium** [Opinion]. Big North American incumbent with no visible focus on Classic AUTOSAR security BSW.

## At a Glance
- **What they do:** Indian ER&D services (L&T Group); Mobility ~30% of revenue (30.1% in Q3 FY26, down from 32.4%); ~80% of mobility revenue from OEMs. [Data]
- **Scale:** 23,830 staff at Q4 FY26 (-220 QoQ; LTM attrition 14.7%); ~USD 306M quarterly revenue (Q4 FY26). [Data]
- **Price / model:** T&M, FTE and large "transformational" deals with dedicated centres (EUR 50M European OEM, Mar 2025 (>12 mo); USD 45M US Tier 1 EV, Jan 2022 (>12 mo)). An LTTS executive says pricing pressure hits "commoditized work", while ER&D pricing is tied to "outcomes, innovation and domain expertise" (2026, low confidence). Fixed-price share DATA GAP. [Data]
- **AI:** AWS GenAI SDV workbench (Oct 2023, >12 mo: "25% faster time-to-launch", CodeWhisperer, 1,000 engineers trained). 2026 agentic AI for automotive: DATA GAP. [Data]
- **Positioning:** ISG Provider Lens Leader for autonomous systems / SDV (North America); partners AWS, BlackBerry QNX, NVIDIA, Qualcomm. AUTOSAR messaging is ADAS-centric. [Data]
- **Best for:** North American OEMs and Tier 1s wanting large SDV/ADAS programmes with a dedicated centre. [Opinion]

## Their Strengths (be honest)
- **Scale (~7k on mobility [Estimate])** and big-deal capability with dedicated centres. [Estimate + Data]
- **Strong North American presence and analyst recognition.** [Data]
- **Recovering:** management says auto "turned the corner"; guides 13-15% revenue CAGR FY27-FY31; JM Financial's top ER&D pick (Oct 2026). [Data]
- **Hyperscaler and silicon ecosystem** (AWS, QNX, NVIDIA, Qualcomm). [Data]

## Their Weaknesses (your openings)
- **Generic AI messaging**; no visible AUTOSAR- or security-BSW-specific AI. [Data, absence]
- **Mobility share was shrinking** through FY26 and growth guidance was cut mid-FY26. [Data]
- **ADAS/SDV focus**, not Classic security integration. [Opinion]
- **Attrition at its functional-safety training academy** (secondary source, low confidence). [Data, Tier 2-3]

## How to Win Against Them
- **Lead with:** a narrow, fixed-price security BSW package that LTTS would not bother to scope separately. [Opinion]
- **Target North American Tier 1s facing R155-driven security retrofits** on Classic ECUs where LTTS is focused on ADAS/HPC. [Opinion]
- **Talking point:** "LTTS builds your SDV platform. We secure the Classic ECUs around it, at a fixed price per ECU." [Opinion]

## When They Win Over You
- Large North American SDV/ADAS programmes needing many engineers and a dedicated centre.
- Accounts with an LTTS MSA where adding scope is frictionless.
- Buyers valuing hyperscaler and QNX ecosystem integration.

## Objections and Responses
- **"LTTS already covers AUTOSAR for us."** Response: "For ADAS, likely. For HSM, SecOC and secure boot on your Classic ECUs, compare our fixed price and evidence with their T&M estimate." [Opinion]
- **"LTTS is an ISG Leader."** Response: "For SDV breadth. We are a specialist for one job, priced per ECU." [Opinion]
